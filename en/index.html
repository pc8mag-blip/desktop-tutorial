Frequently asked questions

Before you ask — I'll answer.

Is this for me if I know nothing about AI?

Yes. First we sort out the business logic. Tools come later, once you know why you're using them. You don't need to be technical — you need to understand the outcome.

Do I need to have a business already?

No. You just need experience, a specific problem to solve, and willingness to make the first move. Businesses are built in practice, not in notebooks.

How much time do I realistically need?

If you can't find 3-5 hours a week, you won't build anything meaningful. But you don't need a free month. You need a system and to cut the noise.

Is the tone on this site the same when we work together?

Yes. No theatre and no sugarcoating. Respectful, but direct. If you prefer a gentle approach — this isn't the right place.

What happens after I submit the form?
